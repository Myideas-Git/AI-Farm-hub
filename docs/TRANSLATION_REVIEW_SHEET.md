## 1. Review Criteria for Human Reviewers

1. **Natural Conversational Telugu (Andhra Pradesh)**: Appropriate, respectful rural Telugu used by Andhra Pradesh farmers (not stiff literal English-to-Telugu translation).
2. **Correct Agricultural Terminology**: Accurate Telugu and Hindi terms for crops, plots, inputs, crop lifecycle stages, quantities, and yields.
3. **Natural Farmer-Friendly Hindi**: Clear, everyday rural Hindi understandable across Hindi-speaking farming communities.
4. **Cross-Screen Consistency**: Consistent vocabulary for repeated concepts (Plot, Demo, Estimate, Confirm, Quintal, Acre, Paddy, Groundnut) across all 5 screens and modals.
5. **Epistemic Precision (`Estimated` vs. `Reported` vs. `Confirmed` vs. `Verified`)**:
   - *Estimated* (`అంచనా` / `अनुमानित`) must never read as actual expenditure or field fact.
   - *Farmer Reported* (`రైతు నమోదు చేశారు` / `किसान द्वारा दर्ज`) must not imply review or third-party proof.
   - *Farmer Confirmed* (`రైతు నిర్ధారించారు` / `किसान द्वारा पुष्ट`) must remain strictly separate from *Independently Verified* (`స్వతంత్రంగా ధృవీకరించబడింది` / `स्वतंत्र रूप से सत्यापित`).
   - Receipts, GPS, weather logs, and photographs must clearly state supporting attachment only, never independent field verification.
6. **Crop Lifecycle, Quantity & Yield Accuracy**: Verify `Yield not recorded` and `Unknown` values never imply zero (`0`).
